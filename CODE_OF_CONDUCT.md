# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and respectful community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive
- Give actionable technical feedback
- Assume good intent
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or exposing private data
- Malicious, deceptive, or abusive behavior
- Sharing exploit details intended for unauthorized harm

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block contributors who violate this policy.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to project maintainers via private contact in repository discussions or security reporting channels.
